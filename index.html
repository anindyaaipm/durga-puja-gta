Durga Puja 2026 dates

Keep this open while you plan. The days below are the Bengali tithi week for 2026, plus the GTA halls that celebrate on the weekends around it. Every association on this page is part of the season — use Show pujas on for the day you can actually go.

Counting down to Shashthi…

Day Date In the GTA
Mahalaya · মহালয়া Sat 10 Oct Many homes play Mahishasura Mardini at dawn. Most GTA halls are not open yet.
Shashthi · ষষ্ঠী Fri 16 Oct Bodhan — the first official puja day. Filter the directory for 16 Oct.
Saptami · সপ্তমী Sat 17 & Sun 18 Oct Two calendar days this year. A good weekend for a same-day itinerary.
Ashtami · অষ্টমী Mon 19 Oct Peak tithi — Pushpanjali and Sandhi Puja. Confirm times with the hall you visit.
Navami · নবমী Tue 20 Oct Last full tithi night. Several GTA pujas still run through this date.
Dashami · দশমী Wed 21 Oct Sindoor Khela and visarjan where a hall observes it. The tithi week closes — GTA pujas continue below.
Weekend after · পরের সপ্তাহ Fri 23 – Sun 25 Oct Same Puja, next available hall dates. These associations belong here too — filter 23–25 Oct.

Tithi week is 16–21 Oct. Saptami covers two sunrises, so Saturday and Sunday both count. Sandhi Puja is the ~48 minutes when Ashtami becomes Navami — ask that hall’s committee for the minutes. In the GTA, venue calendars often land on the weekend after Dashami. That is still Durga Puja, not a leftover.

Day-by-day in the GTA

Mahalaya

Saturday 10 October

Listen once if you can. Then skim the directory for early-weekend pujas (some GTA groups host 10–11 Oct).

Shashthi

Friday 16 October

Add Friday halls to My Puja Plan. One day = an itinerary and a Google Maps route from where you are.

Saptami

Saturday 17 & Sunday 18 October

The busiest GTA weekend. Pick one day, add two or three nearby halls, then Open route. Mixing Saturday and Sunday is a list, not one outing.

Ashtami

Monday 19 October

Go early for anjali if the hall offers it. Sandhi is in the morning this year on the tithi clock — get the exact window from that puja’s committee.

Navami

Tuesday 20 October

Last full tithi for many temples. Finish the weekday halls you skipped on the weekend.

Dashami

Wednesday 21 October

Fewer weekday bookings on a Wednesday. If a hall lists Dashami, it is often visarjan or a short closing — then keep going: the next weekend is still Puja.

The weekend after

Friday 23 – Sunday 25 October

Halls book months ahead, and a Wednesday Dashami is hard for working families. Amantran, Royal City, Tookhar, Utsav London, Niagara Sharodiya, Simcoe Utsav, Mrittika — and anyone else on 23–25 Oct — are on this map for the same reason as the tithi-week halls. Pick one day, add them to My Puja Plan, and open the route.
